# HOS Calculator — User Guide

A plain-English walkthrough of how to use the app every day. If you just want to
get it onto your phone, see the "Getting it onto phones" section of `README.md`.

---

## First launch — set your timezone

The very first time you open the app, a **Welcome** screen asks for your
**Operating Timezone** (Eastern, Central, Mountain, Pacific, etc.).

- Pick the zone you run your clock by and tap **Get Started**.
- This matters because your 8-day log rolls over at **midnight in that zone** —
  that's the moment the oldest day drops off and you "get hours back."
- If you'd rather not set it, tap **Skip** — but then the log won't auto-advance
  at midnight and you'll need to shift days yourself.

You can change the timezone any time by tapping the timezone line near the top
of the screen.

---

## Adjusting text size

At the top there's a **Text size** slider.

- Slide right to enlarge everything, up to **300%**.
- Tap **Reset** next to it to return to 100%.
- The setting sticks, so set it once for comfortable reading in the cab.

## The screen, section by section

### 1. Driver Information
Enter the **Driver Name**. This labels the session and shows up on any data you
export.

### 2. Hours Remaining
This is the headline number: **how many of your 70 hours are still available**.
You don't type here — it updates automatically as you fill in the log below.

### 3. Today's Hours
Enter **Total Hours Worked Today**. This counts against your 70.

### 4. Today's Mileage
Enter **Distance Driven Today** and choose the **Unit** (miles or kilometers).
This is for your own trip records; it doesn't change the hours math.

### 5. 8-Day Driving Log
This is the engine of the 70/8 rule. Enter the hours you worked on **each of the
last 7 days**, plus today. The app adds them up and subtracts from 70 to show
what's left.

- At **midnight** (in your chosen timezone) the log shifts: yesterday becomes
  "day 2 ago," the oldest day falls off, and those hours come back to you. You'll
  see a short "Midnight rollover" message when this happens while the app is open.
- This rolling 8-day window is exactly how the federal rule works — you're never
  limited by a single calendar week, but by any rolling 8 days.

### 6. 70/8 Rule Summary
A quick plain-language recap of where you stand and how the rule is being applied.

> **How the daily limits are calculated:** the 11-hour and 14-hour bars are based
> on the **Total Hours Worked Today** you enter. The 8-day recap ("Hours Gained at
> Midnight") is applied to the 70-hour cycle only — it does **not** add to your
> daily 11/14-hour limits (that would be incorrect under FMCSA rules). Also note
> the real 14-hour limit is a wall-clock window from when you came on duty,
> including breaks; this tool measures it from hours entered, so always confirm
> the actual window against your ELD.

## Resetting

- **Reset** (small button) returns the text size or a single area to default.
- **RESET ALL HOURS & MILEAGE** clears every entry so you can start a clean
  cycle (for example, after a 34-hour restart). It asks before wiping, so an
  accidental tap won't lose your data.

---

## Backing up and moving data between devices

Near the bottom:

- **⬇ Export Data** saves a small backup file of your current driver name, hours,
  and mileage. Keep it somewhere safe, or send it to yourself.
- **⬆ Import Data** loads a backup file back in — handy when you switch phones or
  want to restore after a reset.

Because everything lives on your device, exporting is the only way to copy your
numbers somewhere else. Nothing is ever uploaded automatically.

---

## Using it offline

After you've opened the installed app once with a signal, it works with **no
internet** — in the mountains, in a dead zone, anywhere. Your entries are stored
locally on the device.

---

## A typical day

1. Open the app from your home-screen icon.
2. Glance at **Hours Remaining**.
3. At the end of your driving, update **Today's Hours** and **Today's Mileage**.
4. Tomorrow morning the log has already rolled over — check your new available
   hours and plan the day.
5. After a restart, hit **RESET ALL HOURS & MILEAGE** to begin fresh.

---

## Important reminder

This app is a planning aid, not your legal logbook. Your ELD and your carrier's
records are the official source of truth. Always confirm against them before you
rely on a number here.
