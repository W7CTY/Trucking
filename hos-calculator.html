70/8 Rule Summary

►
11-Hour Drive Limit: Max 11:00 drive after 10 consecutive hours off duty.
►
14-Hour Limit: Cannot drive beyond the 14th hour after coming on duty.
►
70-Hour / 8-Day: Cannot drive after 70:00 on duty in 8 consecutive days.
►
34-Hour Restart: Check RST on a day to restart the 70-hr clock from the next day.
►
Sleeper Berth Split: 8/2 or 7/3 split - shorter period does not count against 14-hr window.
►
30-Min Break: Required after 8:00 driving without a 0:30+ break.
